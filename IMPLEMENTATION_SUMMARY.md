## 📱 How It Works

1. **Allow Location Access** (browser will ask)
2. **Click "Save My Spot"** when parked
3. **App shows distance** to your parking spot (updates in real-time)
4. **Use map** to navigate back to your car
5. **Click "Clear Spot"** once you've found your car

## 📊 Key Algorithms

**Haversine Formula** - Calculates great-circle distance between two GPS points
- Accounts for Earth's spherical shape
- Returns distance in kilometers
- Used for real-time distance updates
